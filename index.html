Personal Assistant

A personal AI assistant that runs on your own computer. Once you connect your Google account, it can read your calendar and email, add events, write drafts and send emails you've confirmed.

It's a private, invite-only project shared with a small number of people. It isn't a commercial service, and there is nothing to buy or sign up for here.

Sign-in uses Google's own page in your browser. Your Google password is never seen by the app. Read the privacy policy and terms.
