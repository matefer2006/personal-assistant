Privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Who runs this

Personal Assistant is a private project run by an individual (contact: matferblan@gmail.com). It's offered free to a small number of invited people. It's not a company and doesn't sell anything.

What Google data the app accesses

With your permission, the app uses these Google scopes: calendar.events (read and change your calendar events), gmail.readonly (read your email), gmail.compose (create drafts and send emails you've confirmed) and gmail.modify (mark email read or unread). It can't permanently delete email.

Where your data goes

  • Your computer. The app runs on your own machine. The sign-in token from Google, and any information the app extracts (for example calendar entries or expense records), are stored locally on that machine. They aren't sent to the person who runs this project, and there is no central server holding your data.
  • AI providers, only to answer your requests. When you ask something that needs your calendar or email (for example "what's on tomorrow?"), the relevant part of it is sent to the AI model answering you: Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or, if you've set them up, Groq or OpenRouter. Only the slice the request needs is sent. These providers' own privacy terms apply.
  • Nobody else. Your Google data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Limited Use

Personal Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

In the app, open Settings › Google › Disconnect to delete the local sign-in token. To revoke access at Google as well, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder on your computer removes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
